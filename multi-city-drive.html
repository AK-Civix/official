About the drive

What is the Multi-City CleanUp Drive?

The Multi-City CleanUp Drive is our flagship quarterly event where volunteers across all six chapters — Pune, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Jamshedpur, Kendrapara, and Puducherry — come together on the same day to clean up public spaces in their cities.

Each city chapter identifies 2-3 high-impact locations (parks, markets, riverbanks, or public squares) and mobilizes local volunteers. Tools, gloves, and bags are provided on-site. After the cleanup, we document the outcomes and follow up with municipal authorities for long-term maintenance.
