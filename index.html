How it works

Three steps to change your neighborhood

01

Spot it. Report it.

See a pothole, garbage pile, or broken streetlight? Snap a photo, pin the location, and submit. Every report is tracked.

02

Neighbors rally.

Volunteers in your area get notified. Tools are gathered. A cleanup drive is organized — sometimes within days.

03

Fixed. Documented. Done.

The issue gets resolved. We document the outcome, follow up with authorities, and close the loop. Impact measured.
