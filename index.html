About CiviX

CiviX empowers citizens to report civic issues in their area, track their resolution, and ensure accountability. From potholes to water leaks and streetlight problems, CiviX connects communities with local authorities.

Our mission: Make civic reporting transparent, fast, and easy for everyone.

How It Works

1. Report

Submit any civic issue with a description and optional media.

2. Track

See your issue on the feed, monitor its status and upvotes.

3. Share

Spread awareness, gather community support and attention.

4. Resolve

Authorities act and the community sees the resolution progress.
