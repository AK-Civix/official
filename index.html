About CiviX

What is CiviX?

CiviX is a platform to report civic issues, track government response, and improve urban life. Your reports make a real difference.

How it Works

Submit an issue with location, description, and optional media. View and upvote others’ reports. Authorities and citizens can track actions.

Why Use CiviX?

It’s simple, fast, and transparent. Every report counts, helping create safer streets, cleaner areas, and accountable governance.
