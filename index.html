About CiviX

CiviX empowers communities to report, track, and resolve civic issues in real-time. Join thousands of citizens making their neighborhoods cleaner, safer, and better maintained.

How It Works

1. Report

Submit issues like potholes, water leaks, or garbage with location & media.

2. Amplify

Community members can view, upvote, and share reported issues.

3. Action

Local authorities and volunteers get notified for resolution.

4. Track

Monitor progress and see which issues have been resolved.
