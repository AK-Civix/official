How We Work

The Civic Action Loop

How we work together to clean up and improve neighborhoods in Pune.

add_a_photo

1. Spot

Citizens report garbage spots, potholes, or broken facilities directly on the platform with photo and location details.

groups

2. Mobilize

We organize local volunteers, gather cleanup supplies (brooms, gloves, bags), and coordinate the schedule.

done_all

3. Resolve

Our community drives clean up the reported issue, document the resolution, and follow up with local authorities.
